\section{Corpus and classification}
\label{sec:corpus}

The preceding sections fixed what matchertext guarantees and at what cost;
the rest of the paper measures how far that guarantee reaches across recorded
injection. That measurement needs a corpus wide enough to be representative and
pinned tightly enough to be reproducible. This section builds one:
\cref{sec:data} unifies the CVE record with enrichment and proof-of-concept
databases, and \cref{sec:classify} labels each injection CVE by the embedded
syntax its attack targets, the axis on which containment turns
(\cref{sec:prevent}).
