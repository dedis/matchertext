\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

The attacker is not adaptive: the recorded payloads target quote- and
tag-delimited host languages, and only the sabotage probes target a hole.
The HTML evidence is one parser, parse5, not a browser, and its oracle reads the
tree, not the JavaScript, URL, or CSS inside it.
Syntax labels are audited on a sample (\cref{sec:classify}).
The projection assumes that the payload-backed CVEs of a syntax represent its
other CVEs.
A stock reader misreads the hole forms (\cref{sec:prevent:html}), and we did
not measure the run-time cost of the forks.
