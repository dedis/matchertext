\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

The hole-targeted attacker is a generated set (\cref{sec:prevent:exec}), not a
search that learns from the parser. Only two host languages are executed. The
browsers are Playwright builds; the HTML oracle reads the tree and one
JavaScript string, not URLs or CSS. Labels are audited on a sample: naive Bayes
has low precision, and the audited delimitable share exceeds the labeled one
(\cref{sec:classify}). One rater graded payloads and delivery rules. The
projection assumes that payload-backed CVEs represent their syntax
(\cref{tab:sens}). Reach counts disclosed CVEs: \dataKevHostableShare of
known-exploited ones are delimitable, and existing defenses cover most
delimitable ones (\cref{sec:prevent}). Stock readers misread holes, an
undeclared charset breaks A3 (\cref{sec:prevent:exec,sec:prevent:html}), and
timings are from one machine. NVD records are inconsistent~\cite{anwar22nvd},
many reports lack what a reproduction needs~\cite{mu18reproducibility}, and
developer studies remain open~\cite{acar16developer}.
