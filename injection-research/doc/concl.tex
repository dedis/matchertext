\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Measured against a reproducible corpus of 91{,}760 injection vulnerabilities,
the matchertext discipline addresses a large and concentrated majority of the
injection landscape: 82\% of attacks for which we could recover a real payload
land in a matcher-delimited slot they cannot escape, and 80\% of all injection
CVEs target a syntax a matchertext host can delimit.
The corpus also confirms the shape of the guarantee rather than only its size:
prevention divides cleanly into inert embedding and outright rejection, and which
mechanism applies is fixed by the host language's native delimiter, exactly as
the structural argument predicts.
The residue is not noise but a precise boundary (execution sinks and
non-matcher-delimited contexts) that matchertext provably cannot address and
that no delimiting discipline could.
The pipeline, corpus manifest, and per-skeleton rewrites are the concrete basis
for extending this measurement beyond the sources studied here and for pairing
the structural argument with the usability questions that adoption ultimately
turns on.
