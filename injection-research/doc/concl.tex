\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Injection is defended today one host language at a time. This paper argues that one
language-independent check can stand in for that collection, and measures how far
it reaches. The guarantee is \emph{inertness}: a host language's parse does not depend on what
its holes contain, a property strictly stronger than closure and discharged by a
machine-checked boundary proof (\cref{sec:appendix:lean}). Where a format already
delimits with matchers, as LDAP filters and PDF strings do, the discipline costs
nothing. Measured against \dataInjectionCVEs injection vulnerabilities,
\dataContainedShare of attacks with a recoverable payload are contained by a
matcher-delimited slot and at most \dataHostableShare target a matcher-delimitable
syntax.
