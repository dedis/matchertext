\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Injection is defended today one host language at a time. This paper argues that one
language-independent check can stand in for that collection, and measures how far
it reaches. The guarantee is \emph{inertness}: a host language's parse does not depend on what
its holes contain. Its boundary premise has a machine-checked proof
(\cref{sec:appendix:lean}). Across \dataInjectionCVEs injection vulnerabilities, the threat model
contains \dataContainedShare of attacks with a recoverable payload in a
matcher-delimited slot, and at most \dataHostableShare target a
matcher-delimitable syntax.
