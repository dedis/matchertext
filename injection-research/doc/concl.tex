\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Injection is defended today one host language at a time. This paper argues that one
language-independent check can stand in for those defenses, and measures how far
it reaches. The guarantee is \emph{inertness}: a host language's parse does not depend on what
its holes contain. Its boundary premise has a machine-checked proof
(\cref{sec:appendix:lean}). Across \dataInjectionCVEs injection CVEs, a
matcher-delimited slot contains \dataContainedShare of attacks with a recoverable
payload, and \dataHostableShare as labeled target a matcher-delimitable syntax.
Matchertext-aware forks of SQLite and parse5 admit no breakout over the recorded
SQL and HTML payloads. Execution sinks and contexts no hole can target stay
out of reach.
