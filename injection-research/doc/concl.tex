\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Injection is defended today one host at a time.
This paper argues that one host-independent check can stand in for that
collection, and measures how far it reaches.
The guarantee is \emph{inertness}: a host's parse does not depend on what its
holes contain.
It is strictly stronger than the closure property the discipline inherits, and
reduces to three premises; the load-bearing boundary premise is discharged by a
machine-checked proof (\cref{sec:appendix:lean}), and the other two are per-host.
Where a format already delimits with matchers, as LDAP filters and PDF strings
do, the discipline costs nothing.
Measured against \dataInjectionCVEs injection vulnerabilities, the reach is a
large majority: \dataContainedShare of attacks with a recoverable payload are
contained by a matcher-delimited slot, and at most \dataHostableShare target a
syntax an aware host can delimit.
