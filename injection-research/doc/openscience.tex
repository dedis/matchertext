\section{Open science}
\label{sec:appendix:openscience}

All artifacts behind this paper's claims are shared at submission through an
anonymized repository (\url{https://anonymous.4open.science/r/matchertext-C533/}), and will be archived publicly
upon publication.
These are the classification pipeline and every generated table and number in
this paper (\cref{sec:corpus}); the corpus manifest pinning each upstream source
by content hash; and the per-skeleton rewrites of the containment measurement
(\cref{sec:prevent:exec}).
They also include the Lean~4 proof developments
(\cref{sec:appendix:lean,sec:appendix:tomatchertext}), and the matchertext-aware
SQLite fork with its driver and test harness.
The pipeline is deterministic, so every quoted figure is reproducible from the
manifest alone.
