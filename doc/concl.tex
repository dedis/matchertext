\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

Matchertext is a syntactic discipline
that enables the verbatim embedding of strings across languages
with no transformation or expansion,
by enforcing only the single rule that the ASCII matchers --
parentheses, square brackets, and curly braces --
must always appear strictly in matched pairs.
Lean~4 proofs establish that embedding preserves matchertext
and that a host finds the end of an embedding by matcher balance alone.
Excluding binary data, \dataStrCompliance of \dataStrSamples string literals are already matchertext,
and matchertext literals would remove \dataReduction of their backslash escapes.
MinML shows that a full markup syntax can rest on the discipline.
Its effect on usability and on the rate of injection bugs
remains open for future work.
