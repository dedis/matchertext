# Constructive submission reviews — 8 November 2026

Reviewed manuscript: *Matchertext: Towards Verbatim Interlanguage Embedding*,
`doc/main.tex` and its included sections and appendices.

Frozen source: commit `c81714bc3f74d3c2d0863f0153d401a879fa1267`
on branch `pldi-2027`. Tracked files were exported to an independent temporary
directory before reviewing. Untracked local files, generated PDFs, and external
submodule contents were not part of that export. References to source lines
refer to this commit, not to future revisions.

These are five simulated reviewer perspectives produced by separate agents,
not independent human reviews, a user study, or official PLDI assessments.
Agents received a common scope and output brief with profile-specific questions.
They did not inherit the earlier conversation or its preliminary critique and
were instructed not to read one another's reviews. Each began with the paper;
targeted artifact inspection followed where appropriate and is labeled in the
individual reports. The separate injection-research paper was outside scope.

The common brief requested a fair contribution reconstruction, strengths,
three principal concerns with source anchors, concrete repairs and completion
criteria, and prioritized improvements spanning writing, design, implementation,
and experiments. Recommendations distinguish submission-critical work,
valuable changes if feasible, and larger post-submission projects.

The submission deadline and available staffing were not supplied. Person-day
estimates are planning judgments, not commitments; corpus access and rerun time
are separate dependencies. No official conference requirements were checked.
The reviews are based on source reading, not a fresh manuscript build, full
experiment rerun, or successful proof rebuild.
